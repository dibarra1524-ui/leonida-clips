# Rules for the daily episode

Written by the review run. Every gauntlet verdict goes under Verdicts; a fault that repeats 3+ times becomes a Rule.

## Rules
- Open on the strongest clip of the day. No intro, no talking head, no "welcome back".
- Every clip has a one-line label and the streamer's handle; every streamer is linked in the description.
- No sexual content anywhere in the episode or thumbnail (strip clubs, nude/topless NPCs), even in the background.
- No story spoilers, endings, cutscenes, exploits or glitch tutorials.
- Thumbnail text: 2-3 big words, readable at phone size, never something that is not in the episode.
- The opener must show its payoff in the first few seconds (crash, bail, reveal); a clip that starts with calm driving goes later, whatever its views.
- Thumbnails: zoom-crop the base frame so no facecam fragments, chat, stream timers or twitch handle boxes show; the arrow/circle sits on the person or object doing the action.
- Scan whole clips, not one frame: billboards and ads for strip clubs (pole dancers) count as sexual content and drop the clip.
