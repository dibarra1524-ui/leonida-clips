# What the outlier thumbnails and titles have in common

Rewritten 2026-11-23 (Monday review) from research/outliers.csv (scan of 2026-11-19, 55 long-form outliers, 20 channels)
and research/thumbs/. The Monday 7:23 outlier scan had not run yet at review time, so this uses last week's data.

## Thumbnails
- **Text is short or absent.** Most top outliers (67.7x military base, 22.9x prison scene, 15.5x car heist, 6.8x airplane)
  have NO text at all - just one clear, sharp, bright game frame. When there is text it is 1-3 words: "#1", "PART 1",
  "TOP 200!", "50 NEW FACTS", "NO SPOILERS", "WHICH IS BEST?", "VS".
- **Text style:** heavy white or yellow block letters with a thick black outline/shadow, or white on a red/black box.
  Placed in a corner, never over the subject.
- **One subject, big and centred.** A single car, a character, a giant object (mask, eye). Nothing cluttered.
- **Bright saturated daylight** dominates: blue sky, teal water, orange sand, red/pink cars. Night thumbs work only with
  one strong light source (King Crane horror series).
- **Faces:** character faces (Lucia, Jason, Franklin) close up and emotional. No real streamer webcams in the outliers.
- **Cars + cops:** a red car with a police car / crash is a recurring winner (car heist 15.5x, military base 67.7x).
- **Arrows/circles:** none of the top outliers use red arrows or circles. Prefer `none`; use an arrow only if the
  action is small in the frame.
- **Branding:** many stamp the GTA VI logo top-left - it signals "GTA 6" at a glance.
- **"VS" split frames** win for comparison content (24.7x, 8.5x, 6.6x) - not relevant to clip compilations.

## Titles
- Lead with **GTA 6** or make it obvious in the first 3 words.
- Concrete noun + action: "Car Heist and a high-speed Chase with the Cops", "Military Base ... First Look".
- Curiosity with a question/!? : "What LEFT This GIANT MASK in GTA 5?!".
- Numbers and lists: "TOP 200", "50 NEW Confirmed Facts", "13 THINGS".
- CAPS on 1-2 key words only, not the whole title.
- Compilations that win: "TOP 100 FUNNIEST GTA 5 FAILS! (Best GTA 5 Funny Moments)", "CRAZY JUMPS & BEST FALLS #8" -
  numbered series + superlative + "Funny Moments/Fails".
- Median winning compilation length: ~17 minutes (our episodes are 3-5 min; longer would match the winners better).
